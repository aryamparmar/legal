Claim.io Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Claim.io ("the game", "we") is a single-player arcade game for Android published by Aryam Parmar. This policy explains what data the game uses.

What stays on your device

Your progress (coins, unlocked skins, chosen map, settings, the name you type for yourself) is saved on your device only. We have no accounts, no login and no server that receives your game data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes this data.

Ads, purchases and analytics

This version of the game has no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not use your advertising ID. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information, and we do not sell or share personal data. The game plays entirely from files inside the app and needs no internet connection.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
