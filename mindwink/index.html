Mindwink Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Mindwink ("the game", "we") is a single-player puzzle game for Android published by Aryam. This policy explains what data the game uses.

What stays on your device

Your progress (current level, solved levels, coins, ad timing counters and whether you bought Remove Ads) is saved on your device only. We have no accounts, no login and no server that receives your game data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes this data.

Some puzzles ask you to shake or tilt your phone. The game reads the motion sensors only while you play, on your device, to detect the shake or tilt; these readings are not stored or sent anywhere.

Ads

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob: an optional rewarded video you choose to watch for free coins, and an interstitial ad between some levels. To show ads, Google AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. We do not receive or store this data. How Google uses it is described at https://policies.google.com/privacy and in Google's "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" page (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game shows Google's consent form before ads load, and you can choose not to allow personalised ads. You can change that choice later under Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Purchases

The game offers one optional one-time purchase, "Remove Ads". Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google Play tells the game whether you own the purchase so it can be restored on a new device (Settings > Restore purchases).

Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and we do not sell or share personal data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
